Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to see Ronen's work and get in touch, including people with disabilities. This site was built to meet Israeli Standard 5568 (based on the international WCAG guidelines, level AA), as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

What we have done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Pages are built with proper headings, labelled buttons and links, so screen readers can announce them.
  • Every piece of jewelry has a written name and description; videos and 3D views have text labels.
  • Text and background colours meet the AA contrast requirement.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and zoom up to 200% without losing content.
  • Moving content (looping videos and the 3D ring) can be paused, and it stops automatically on devices set to reduce motion.
  • An accessibility menu (the blue button at the bottom left) lets you pause animation, enlarge text, increase contrast, underline links and switch to a plain font. Your choices are remembered on your device.

Known limitations

  • The videos have no sound and no spoken content, so they have no captions.
  • Links to Instagram and WhatsApp open services that are not run by us, and their accessibility is the responsibility of those services.

The site was checked with automated testing (axe) and by hand. If you find something we missed, please tell us.
